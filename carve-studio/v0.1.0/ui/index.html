V-bit Inlay

Creates a female V-carved pocket and a matching mirrored plug from the selected closed lettering or shapes. Save and cut each half on its own stock setup.

Feed, plunge, and spindle speed come from the selected V-bit in the tool library. The pocket V-cuts the selected shapes to a flat floor. The mirrored plug V-cuts the surrounding field, leaving raised inlay shapes. The plug border is a rectangular machining boundary; profile it separately to free the plug.
