Perimeter probing mode

Each probe releases by moving outward 0.75 × the tip diameter. Between perimeter points, the tool moves laterally at probing height; Z is raised for initial approach, completion, or recovery.

The search box is centered on the approximate part-center WPOS origin and must contain the outline plus the calculated margin. Current perimeter assumptions
  • One connected outer boundary, captured at one Z height.
  • Features narrower than 4 × tip diameter may be missed.
  • The 3D probe can reach the edge and physically release after every touch.
  • Narrow-mouth bays are only supported when the tip and usable shaft physically fit through the opening.
